# Changelog

## 2.0.0
- Renamed to grademapper (your saved data is kept; storage keys are unchanged).
- Every theme now uses the Academic theme's text and card size. The text-size slider tops out at 130%.
- Standard theme is black on off-white (dark mode: off-white on near-black).
- New themes: Paper (Uncial Antiqua headings, periwinkle and dark clay) and Avocado Smoothie (Syne Mono, olive, pastel plum).
- Pink Flowey: falling petals removed; a still flower pattern replaces them.
- Tools moved to a button in the top-right corner. They open in a panel over any page, and a small popup shows timers, sounds, recording and reading aloud while they run.
- Sounds: six in total (White noise, Brown noise, Rain, Ocean, Plane noise, Forest). Rain, Ocean and Forest use recordings from `audio/` when present.
- New "To come" box in every category: type how many scores are still to come (for example 3 tests left) and the "What do I need?" number updates. Nothing in your saved data changes shape.
- Pressing a button anywhere no longer jumps you back to the top.
- Archive for earlier classes, grades and GPA.
- Welcome: country first, then the scales for that country (17 countries); animated tips show where to read and tap.
- Backups from version 1 still restore. Backups made by version 2 are labelled grademapper.
