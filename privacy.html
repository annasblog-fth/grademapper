Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

grademapper is built so that your grades stay yours. Here is exactly what happens with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. The people who make grademapper never receive your grades, notes, activities, name or goals.

Where your information lives

  • Everything you type (courses, scores, activities, notes, goals, settings) is saved on your own device, inside this app's storage in your browser.
  • You can lock it with a passphrase. It is then encrypted on your device. We cannot reset a forgotten passphrase.
  • You can delete everything at any time with the "Delete everything" button in Settings.
  • Backup files you download are saved wherever you choose. They are encrypted only if you set a passphrase for them.

Things that involve other companies

  • Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser makes a normal web request to Google, which can include your IP address. No grades or personal information are sent. After the first visit the fonts are saved for offline use.
  • Speech to text. If you use "Lecture to text", your browser's own speech service turns your voice into words. In many browsers (such as Chrome, Edge and Safari) that service sends the audio to the browser maker's servers. grademapper never receives or stores the audio. Skip this tool if that is not okay for your class or your school.
  • Reminders. Reminders are created on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children and students

Because grademapper collects no personal information, there is nothing about a child to collect, store or share. If you are under 18, you can use it without an account.
